## Pinoy Big Brother: Collab 3.0 Update: Happy Birthday, Shea and Lans!

Within the first week of Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0's run, Shea Valerio and Lans Nagal had celebrated their birthdays during their stay inside the newly-built Bahay ni Kuya!

Shea Valerio recently turned 20 years old on October 27, 2026, with a birthday surprise from Piolo and Inigo Pascual, Kyle Echarri, River Joseph, Emilio Daez, and Darren Espanto, as seen in the October 29, 2026 episode of Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0.

For the October 30, 2026 episode, experience the Halloween feels of Shea and today's birthday celebrant, Lans Nagal, who turned 24 today (born October 30, 2002) inside the Bahay ni Kuya, later at 21:35 (after Taskforce Firewall) on Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0.
