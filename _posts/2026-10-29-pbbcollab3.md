## Pinoy Big Brother: Collab 3.0 Houseguests: October 29, 2026

As part of Shea's birthday last October 27, 2026 (born 2006, aged 20), some of the ex-housemates of Pinoy Big Brother and various artists will appear as the houseguests, while their first-ever weekly task of the season, titled "The Big Foundation" is currently ongoing.

For the October 29, 2026 broadcast of PBB Collab 3.0, viewers will expect the following artists to appear as houseguests:

- Kyle Echarri (ex-housemate, Pinoy Big Brother Kumunity Season 10 - Celebrity Edition; TV and film actor)
- Darren Espanto (singer-songwriter, ABS-CBN Music)
- Piolo Pascual (TV and film actor)
- Inigo Pascual (brother of Piolo, singer-songwriter)
- Emilio Daez (Kapamilya ex-housemate, Pinoy Big Brother Celebrity Collab Edition 1.0; TV and film actor)
- River Joseph (Kapamilya ex-housemate, Pinoy Big Brother Celebrity Collab Edition 1.0; TV and film actor)

Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0 airs on Saturdays at 18:15, Sundays at 16:55, and Weekdays at 21:35, via GMA, Kapuso Stream, ABS-CBN Entertainment, and Kapamilya Online Live. Viewers may also watch the All-Access Live Streaming on the iWant app and website; subscribers may also stream the Dining Area and External Den live stream through its subscription plans priced locally at PHP 35.00 per month in the Philippines, or USD 4.99 per month elsewhere.
