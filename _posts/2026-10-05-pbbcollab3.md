## PBB Collab 3.0: Michael Sager joins Pamilya Ni Kuya for PBB Collab 3.0

![Michael Sager of PBB Collab 1.0 joins Pamilya Ni Kuya for PBB Collab 3.0](https://pbbupdatescdn.drpsphca.com/img/blogs/PBBCollab-Collab3QuickUpdate-MichaelSagerJoinsPamilyaNiKuya-20261005.avif){: width="1200" height="1250" }

Pinoy Big Brother Celebrity Collab Edition 1.0 ex-housemate and Sparkle Actor Michael Sager, recently announced during 24 Oras that he is now part of the Big Brother's family and to host for the upcoming season of Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0, scheduled to premiere this October 2026.

Apart from Michael, PBB Collab 3.0 is hosted by Robi Domingo, Bianca Gonzalez, Melai Cantiveros-Francisco, Enchong Dee, Alexa Ilacad, and Gabbi Garcia.

Michael, together with ex-housemates AZ Martinez and Ralph de Leon will appear in an upcoming Viu Original Series, Way Back To You.

PBB Collab 3.0 will feature a new set of civillian housemates aged 18 to 35 done through online auditions via the ABS-CBN Join Now website, living inside in the new Bahay Ni Kuya, situated in an undisclosed location, as of this writing.
