## EN: PBB Collab 3.0 to stop livestreams to protect Housemates (November 21, 2026)

ABS-CBN and GMA opened the livestream from the Pinoy Big Brother house to give our audience the opportunity to get to know the Housemates better, hear more about their journeys, and foster deeper understanding — allowing both the Housemates and viewers alike to learn from their shared life stories.

Unfortunately, irresponsible netizens have misused or taken advantage of the livestream to maliciously create false stories that are libelous. These twisted narratives that malign some of the Housemates and instigate hate against them spread a toxic environment online.

It is wrong to maliciously edit videos from the livestream that take the words and actions of our Housemates out of context. These videos are irresponsibly shared on social media even before Big Brother can process and guide our Housemates.

For the safety and well-being of our Housemates and their families, ABS-CBN and GMA have decided to stop all livestreams of “Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0” effective tonight (21 Nov 2026).

ABS-CBN and GMA condemn cyberbullying, coordinated online harassment, threats, and abuse in social media.

We encourage everyone to show kindness and respect for our Housemates and for one another. Together, let us enjoy “Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0” and create a positive and healthy social media environment.

Viewers can continue to follow the journey of our Housemates through our regular episodes, official digital content, and the new “iWant PBB Extra.”

We thank our viewers for their understanding and continued support.

## TL: PBB Collab 3.0, ititigil ang livestream para protektahan ang Housemates

Binuksan ng ABS-CBN and GMA ang livestream mula sa Bahay ni Kuya para mas makilala ng mga manonood ang Housemates, malaman ang kanilang mga kuwento, maintindihan at matuto sa mga pinagdaanan nila sa buhay.

Ngunit may mga iresponsableng netizens na inaabuso at sinasamantala ang livestream para gumawa ng mali, malisyoso at mapanirang kuwento tungkol sa Housemates na maituturing nang libelous.

Hindi tama ang pagbabaluktot sa mga kuwento, pagbibigay ng masamang kahulugan sa mga sinasabi ng mga Housemate at paghuhusga sa kanila on social media.

Ang pag-edit at pag-share ng mga video mula sa livestream kahit hindi pa nagkakaroon ng pagkakataon si Big Brother na kausapin at gabayan ang Housemates ay hindi nagpapakita ng kabuuan ng kwento.

Para sa kaligtasan at kapakanan ng Housemates at kanilang mga pamilya, nagdesisyon ang ABS-CBN at GMA na itigil ang lahat ng livestream ng “Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0” simula ngayong gabi (November 21, 2026).

Kinokondena namin ang cyberbullying, paninira ng pagkatao, sama-samang pangha-harass, pagbabanta at pagpapalaganap ng galit, negativity at toxicity sa social media.

Hinihikayat namin ang lahat na magpakita ng kabutihan at respeto para sa lahat ng mga Housemate at para sa isa’t isa.

Sama sama tayong mag-enjoy sa panonood ng “Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0” at gawing positibo at good vibes ang social media interactions ng lahat.

Patuloy nating subaybayan ang kuwento ng mga Housemate sa regular na “PBB” episodes, official digital content at sa bagong “iWant PBB Extra.”

Maraming salamat sa inyong pag-unawa at patuloy na suporta.
